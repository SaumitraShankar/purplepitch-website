How we think

Four I's. One bright idea.

The four lamps in our logo are the four I's. An idea switches them on in order, and when all four are lit it is ready for prime time.

  1. 01

    Intent · the why

    Set a clear objective before anything else.

  2. 02

    Identify · the who

    Know the audience: their needs, their motivations and why they will care.

  3. 03

    Imagine · the what

    Explore every possibility without limits, then build it for the platform and the budget.

  4. 04

    Ignite · the feeling

    Create the emotion, inspiration and memories people carry away.
